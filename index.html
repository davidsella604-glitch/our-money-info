A private dashboard for reviewing the owner’s QuickBooks and household records.

Public information. Private financial data.

Authorized access only

The local service is designed to retrieve authorized QuickBooks information without posting, editing, deleting or reconciling accounting records. It does not initiate payments or actual money transfers.

Connection status

Publishing this information does not activate a QuickBooks connection. Production approval, owner authorization and a verified first read are separate requirements.
