Privacy policy

Our Money is a private, personal-use application operated by David Sella. It is not offered as a public financial service and has no public user registration.

Information used

With the owner’s authorization, the local service reads QuickBooks account names, transaction identifiers, dates, amounts, counterparties, descriptions, categories and financial reports. Dashboard-specific attributions and exclusions are separate from QuickBooks. Unlinked household accounts are outside coverage.

Where data goes

The dashboard and synchronization process run on the owner’s Mac. The connector communicates with Intuit to authorize access and read records. Source exports and continuity evidence may be retained in the owner’s private Google Drive project. No financial records, balances, authorization codes or credentials are published to this website or its GitHub repository.

Credentials and access

The connector stores configured authorization credentials in macOS Keychain, outside the dashboard HTML, source repository and Drive evidence. Local cache access is restricted to the owner’s account; its local HTTP endpoint checks an access key and the requesting origin. These controls do not substitute for the owner’s Mac security or backup settings.

Public website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts these informational pages and may process visitors’ connection metadata under GitHub’s privacy statement. No advertising, tracking scripts, analytics forms or financial data collection are included here. Intuit and Google process information through their own services and policies.

Retention and control

The owner controls local snapshots and private project evidence. Records remain until the owner removes them; disconnecting QuickBooks does not automatically erase previous exports or backups. The owner can stop the service and revoke access through Intuit. Data is not sold.
