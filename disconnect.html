Disconnect

To revoke the dashboard’s access, the owner can open QuickBooks, go to its connected-app management and disconnect Our Money Dashboard. The local synchronization process can also be stopped on the Mac.

Stopping a process alone does not revoke an existing Intuit authorization. Likewise, revoking authorization does not automatically delete earlier downloaded snapshots, source exports or private project evidence.

This public page does not receive credentials or perform account changes. Open your Intuit account to manage account access.
