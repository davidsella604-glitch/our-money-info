Private-use terms

Our Money Dashboard is intended solely for David Sella’s authorized review of his company and household information. It is not a public service, financial institution, payroll system or accounting practice.

Read-only connection

The local connector is designed to retrieve authorized QuickBooks information for display. It provides no accounting write, delete, reconciliation, payment or actual-transfer operation. Dashboard classifications do not alter QuickBooks.

Interpretation and limitations

Recorded deposits are not automatically earnings, and recorded outflows are not automatically household consumption. Transfers, investment movements, opening balances, reversals and reimbursements require separate interpretation. Missing or unreviewed information must be identified; incomplete contribution records are not a complete wage history.

The dashboard is not a tax return, completed bank reconciliation, investment recommendation, audited financial statement or guarantee of funds available to spend. Source records and unresolved review items remain necessary to interpret its figures.

Authorization and ending use

The account owner controls authorization and may revoke Intuit access or stop the local application. Previously downloaded records and private project evidence remain until removed by the owner. No dashboard subscription or paid service is offered by this private application. Separate Intuit and hosting-provider terms remain applicable.
